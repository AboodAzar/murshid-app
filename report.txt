<p><strong>Graduation Project Report: Murshid (مرشد)</strong></p><p><strong>Intelligent Academic Advising Platform</strong></p><p><strong>By: Abdualrhman Abed &amp; Noor Al-Huda Hussain</strong></p><p><strong>1. Introduction</strong></p><p>The <strong>Murshid</strong> project is an intelligent, web-based academic advising platform designed specifically for the students and faculty at Al-Zaytoonah University of Jordan. By leveraging modern web technologies and advanced Artificial Intelligence (AI), Murshid acts as a 24/7 personalized academic advisor. It aims to streamline the advising process, empower students to track their academic progress independently, and relieve the administrative burden on university staff.</p><p><strong>2. The Problem Statement</strong></p><p>In the traditional academic advising system, several inefficiencies negatively impact both students and faculty:</p><ul><li><strong>Repetitive Inquiries:</strong> University doctors and academic advisors spend a significant amount of their valuable time answering the exact same routine questions from hundreds of students (e.g., registration dates, prerequisite inquiries, and graduation requirements).</li><li><strong>Misallocation of Time:</strong> Because advisors are bogged down by these repetitive questions, they have less time to provide real, in-depth support to students who are facing complex academic or personal challenges that actually require human intervention.</li><li><strong>Limited Availability:</strong> Human advisors are only available during specific office hours, leaving students without immediate guidance when they are planning their schedules or making urgent academic decisions late at night or on weekends.</li></ul><p><strong>3. The Solution</strong></p><p>To solve these problems, we developed <strong>Murshid</strong>, a platform that automates routine academic advising through an intelligent AI chatbot, paired with a comprehensive student dashboard.</p><ul><li><strong>Automated Advising:</strong> Murshid instantly answers repetitive student questions accurately, freeing up university doctors to focus on students who need genuine human help.</li><li><strong>Personalized Dashboards:</strong> Students log in to see their real-time GPA, completed hours, remaining semesters, and enrolled courses, reducing the need to ask advisors for basic academic standing information.</li><li><strong>Secure and Official:</strong> The platform integrates a secure authentication system that strictly requires an official university email (@zuj.edu.jo or @std-zuj.edu.jo) to ensure privacy and exclusivity.</li></ul><p><strong>4. Technical Architecture and Model Selection</strong></p><p>Choosing the Right AI Model is one of the most critical decisions in this project was selecting the underlying AI model. The AI landscape is vast, and each model has its own strengths and weaknesses. After rigorous testing, we selected the <strong>Qwen 3.6</strong> model for several compelling reasons:</p><ol><li><strong>Massive Context Window:</strong> It supports a context window of up to 1 million tokens. This is crucial because it allows the model to remember and understand the student's active conversation seamlessly.</li><li><strong>Multimodal Capabilities:</strong> Its modern architecture supports both text and image processing. While the current version of Murshid focuses on text, this capability ensures the platform is future-proof and ready for image manipulation features planned for Version 2.</li><li><strong>Concurrent Usage:</strong> The system was engineered and hosted to support multiple students using the AI model at the exact same time without performance degradation.</li></ol><p>Multi-Agent System</p><p>To ensure the AI strictly adheres to university guidelines, we built a highly optimized, multi-agent architecture rather than relying on a single prompt:</p><ul><li><strong>Data Agent:</strong> Before the main model answers, a Data Agent searches and retrieves the most relevant university data, feeding the best-fit information to the main model to ensure high accuracy and fast data retrieval.</li><li><strong>Reroute Agent: </strong>A specialized agent continuously monitors the conversation. If a student tries to change the subject or go off-topic, the Reroute Agent ensures the AI samslly change the topic within the app use case and also well prevent it from hallucination.</li><li><strong>Robust Prompt Engineering:</strong> We crafted highly detailed system prompts so that the model consistently delivers excellent, natural-sounding responses, regardless of whether the student types a well-structured question or a poorly phrased one.</li></ul><p><strong>5. Challenges and Difficulties Faced</strong></p><p>Building a production-ready AI platform from scratch presented several major challenges that we successfully overcame:</p><ul><li><strong>Controlling AI Hallucinations:</strong> The toughest challenge was optimizing the prompts to ensure the model acted naturally while strictly preventing it from "hallucinating" or answering questions outside the provided university data. We solved this through the multi-agent system described above.</li><li><strong>Cloud Deployment and Accessibility:</strong> We wanted the project to be easily accessible over the internet rather than just running locally. We successfully purchased a domain name and configured <strong>Google Cloud</strong> to host the project, ensuring a reliable, public-facing web infrastructure.</li><li><strong>Database Reconnections:</strong> Transitioning the database from a local environment to a live, secure Google Cloud SQL instance required careful configuration of environment variables to maintain stable, persistent connections.</li><li><strong>Right-to-Left (RTL) UI Consistency:</strong> Ensuring the user interface looked perfect and did not break when switching between English and Arabic required rewriting the styling using modern CSS logical properties.</li><li><strong>Official Email Verification:</strong> Integrating the Resend email system to securely deliver One-Time Passwords (OTPs) while enforcing that only students with verified university emails could register required complex backend validation logic.</li></ul><p><strong>6. Conclusion</strong></p><p>The Murshid platform successfully demonstrates how modern AI can be practically applied within an educational institution. By handling routine inquiries, Murshid not only provides students with immediate, accurate, and personalized academic guidance, but it also restores valuable time to university doctors, allowing them to focus on meaningful mentorship and complex student needs.</p>
